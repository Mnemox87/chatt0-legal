1. THE SHORT VERSION

We do not collect your personal data. There is no server, no account
database, no analytics and no telemetry. Nothing you type, say or send
reaches us, because there is nowhere for it to reach.

Everything below is detail on that, and on what stays on your own machine.

2. WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing.

The Software has no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no
advertising identifiers and no tracking of any kind. We do not know who
uses it, when, or from where. We do not have an account for you.

3. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

All of the following is stored locally, under your operating system user
account, and is never transmitted to us:

  (a) Your account: your email address, an encrypted copy of your identity
      key, and (only if you tick "sign me in automatically") your password
      protected by your operating system's credential store.
  (b) Your profile: display name, status message, picture, colour choices.
  (c) Your contacts and rooms, including their Chatt0 addresses.
  (d) Your conversation history, including message text, pictures and voice
      clips, kept until you delete it.
  (e) Records of content the local filter flagged, including the text that
      was flagged and the address it came from. This is your own record of
      what was sent to you.
  (f) Device preferences: language, window style, and the fact that you
      accepted these terms and confirmed your age.

We do not store your date of birth. When you confirm your age, only the fact
that the check passed is recorded, not the date you entered.

Removing your account from the sign-in screen deletes all of the above for
that account. Uninstalling the Software and deleting its application data
removes the rest.

4. WHAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE, AND WHERE IT GOES

  (a) To the people you communicate with, directly: your messages, calls,
      pictures, voice clips, display name, status message, profile picture
      and Chatt0 address. This travels directly between your device and
      theirs. It does not pass through us.
  (b) To the public distributed hash table: your Chatt0 address and network
      address, so that people you contact can find and connect to you. This
      is a public peer-to-peer network operated by nobody in particular. Be
      aware that participating in it reveals your IP address to the peers
      you connect to, in the same way that any direct connection does.
That is the complete list. The Software makes no other network request of
any kind -- it does not check for updates, fetch remote content, or contact
any service operated by us or by anyone else.

5. WHAT OTHER USERS RECEIVE ABOUT YOU

Anyone you accept as a contact receives your display name, status message,
profile picture, name colour and online status, and can see your IP address
by virtue of being connected to you directly. Anyone who knows your Chatt0
address can attempt to contact you; you choose whether to accept.

Once a message reaches another person's device it is on their device. We
cannot recall, alter or delete it, and neither can you.

6. CHILDREN

The Software is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children
and we do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to use it. We hold no data
about anyone, so there is no children's data for us to hold or delete.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

The controller named at the top of this policy is established in Brazil, so
this policy is governed by the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (Lei nº
13.709/2018, the "LGPD").

The LGPD gives you the right to ask the controller to confirm that
processing exists; to access your data; to correct incomplete or outdated
data; to anonymise, block or delete data that is unnecessary or processed
unlawfully; to port your data to another provider; to delete data processed
on the basis of consent; to be told with whom your data has been shared;
and to object to processing.

We hold no personal data about you. There is no server, no account database
and no record that you exist, so for every one of those requests the honest
answer is that we have nothing to confirm, produce, correct, port or erase.
Your data is on your own device and entirely under your control: you can
read it, export it and delete it yourself at any time.

Requests and questions go to joaoantoniofbasso@gmail.com, which is the
controller's channel for communication with data subjects, and we will
answer within the period the LGPD requires. You may also complain to the
Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

If you are outside Brazil, the law where you live may give you equivalent
rights -- the GDPR in the European Union, the UK GDPR in the United
Kingdom, and comparable regimes elsewhere. Our answer does not change: we
hold nothing about you, and we will respond on the same terms.

8. SECURITY

Your identity key is encrypted on your device using scrypt and AES-256-GCM,
protected by your password. Your conversation history is stored unencrypted
under your operating system user account, and is protected by your device's
own security. If other people can use your computer, they can read your
messages. Use full-disk encryption and a device password.

9. CHANGES

We may update this policy in a future version of the Software. Material
changes will be presented before you continue using that version.
